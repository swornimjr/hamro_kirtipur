Usage

Do

  • Keep the circular photo logo as the main mark
  • Use hill green for primary actions and active tabs
  • Use sunset orange for brand accent and emergency
  • Use warm sand sparingly for badges
  • Pair Devanagari (Mukta) with Latin (Outfit)

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, or recolour the logo
  • Crop the circular border off the logo
  • Use orange for large background areas
  • Mix in the old earthy/forest palette
  • Place the logo on busy photo backgrounds
